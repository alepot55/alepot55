## The Role

Since April 2026 I have been a Visiting AI Engineer at BCG X, the build-and-design arm of Boston Consulting Group, based in Milan. The work sits at the intersection of strategy and engineering: I advise enterprise clients in luxury retail and industrial sectors on where generative AI actually creates value, prioritize the high-impact use cases, and then design and deploy the solutions end to end.

In practice that means multi-agent automation on Google Cloud and Vertex AI, and LLM-based content generation, taken from a slide to a running system rather than a proof of concept that never ships.

## Content Studio

The work I am most proud of is Content Studio. It started as a single client engagement, and I productized it into a reusable platform that is now adopted across multiple customers, including Burberry and Prada. Turning a one-off build into something repeatable is a different discipline from delivering a single project: it forces clean abstractions, sane defaults, and an interface a new team can adopt without me in the room.

## Bridging Strategy and Engineering

A large part of the value is translation. I act as the bridge between executive stakeholders, who think in business outcomes, and engineering teams, who think in systems and constraints. Keeping both sides aligned is what turns an AI ambition into something that actually ships.

I also run an internal "Tech Hour", upskilling senior engineers on agentic AI: how agents are built, where they break, and how to evaluate them honestly.
