## In short

- MSc in Computer Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, chosen after graduating cum laude from Sapienza for its focus on systems engineering and its proximity to the European AI research ecosystem.
- The program pairs depth in machine learning theory with hands-on high-performance computing, which is exactly the combination I wanted.
- My thesis sits inside the EU Perivallon Horizon Europe project: deep learning for satellite imagery, specifically efficient neural architectures for environmental monitoring from remote sensing data.
- Along the way: a merit-based scholarship and 3rd place out of 193 teams in the AIRLab Artificial Neural Networks Challenge.

## Focus areas

- **Deep learning and representation learning**: advanced architectures, training dynamics, generalization theory
- **Computer vision and 3D reconstruction**: building on my bachelor's thesis work with Gaussian Splatting
- **High-performance computing**: GPU acceleration, distributed training, kernel optimization in Triton and CUDA
- **Systems design**: scalable architectures for ML inference and training pipelines

## Thesis

The work is part of the EU Perivallon Horizon Europe project and focuses on deep learning for satellite imagery analysis.

The research develops efficient neural architectures for environmental monitoring from remote sensing data.

## Achievements during the program

- **Merit-based scholarship** awarded for outstanding academic performance, on GPA and credits
- **3rd place in the Artificial Neural Networks Challenge (AIRLab)**, ranked 3rd out of 193 teams with a custom Vision Transformer ensemble for medical image classification
- Active participation in the AI research community at Polimi's AIRLab

## What the coursework turned into

The courses gave me the theoretical foundation and the projects are where it comes together.
