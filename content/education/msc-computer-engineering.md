## The thesis measures how localisation degrades with satellite resolution

It sits inside PERIVALLON, a Horizon Europe project. Seventy logged experiments ask how far a weakly supervised classifier can point at an illegal landfill as ground resolution drops from 30 cm to 120 cm. The result is a readout law and a retraining-free lever that doubles recall at 120 cm. It is written up as its own project on this site.
