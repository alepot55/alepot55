# Design system in use (read before touching any component)

Repo root: `/home/alepot55/Desktop/projects/portfolio/.claude/worktrees/portfolio-update-design-9b6145`

The site was just rebuilt on six semantic colour tokens and two typefaces. Every
component must speak that language. Nothing here is optional.

## Colour: six tokens, no others

Defined in `app/globals.css` as HSL triplets, exposed to Tailwind in
`tailwind.config.js` as `bg`, `surface`, `ink`, `ref`, `rail`, `limit`.

| token | role | Tailwind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--bg` | page field | `bg-bg` |
| `--surface` | header bar, code blocks, chart frames | `bg-surface` |
| `--ink` | primary text, measured values, filled segments | `text-ink`, `bg-ink` |
| `--ref` | metadata, units, labels, axes | `text-ref`, `bg-ref` |
| `--rail` | decorative hairlines only, never information | `border-rail` |
| `--limit` | constraints only: physical limits, focus ring, active state | `text-limit`, `bg-limit` |

Hard rules:

- **No `gray-*`, `slate-*`, `zinc-*`, `blue-*`, `purple-*`, `emerald-*`, `amber-*`,
  `rose-*` or any other Tailwind palette colour.** Not in light mode, not in dark
  mode. Every colour comes from the six tokens.
- **No `dark:` variants for colour.** The tokens flip themselves via the `.dark`
  class on `<html>`. A `dark:` colour class is a bug.
- `--limit` marks what something is measured against, never a success state. A
  value that beats its limit is the only exception, and only in `components/measure.tsx`.
- `--rail` never carries information a reader must see (contrast is ~1.2:1).
- Need a subtle fill? `bg-ink/[0.05]`. Need a subtle hover? `hover:bg-ink/[0.04]`.
  Never a new colour.

## Type: two families, one rule

- `font-mono` (Chivo Mono) for anything that is a label, an identifier, a number,
  a unit, a heading, a nav item, a chart tick, or code.
- default sans (Geist) for sentences only.
- **Every visible digit on the site is in mono.** No exceptions outside KaTeX.
- Sizes come from the scale in `tailwind.config.js`: `text-micro`, `text-meta`,
  `text-unit`, `text-nav`, `text-body`, `text-lead`, `text-index`, `text-row-title`,
  `text-value-s|m|xl`. Do not invent sizes with arbitrary values unless a clamp is
  genuinely needed.
- Spaced uppercase (`uppercase tracking-micro`) survives in exactly two places: the
  hero eyebrow and the artifact line in `ValueCell`. Do not add a third. Section
  headers are sentence case.
- Weights available: 400, 500, 600. `font-bold` is not available; use `font-semibold`.

## Surfaces and lines

- **No cards.** No `border` boxes around content, no `shadow-*` in any state, no
  gradients.
- Radius is 4px everywhere (`rounded`, `rounded-md`, `rounded-lg` all resolve to
  4px; `rounded-full` too). `rounded-sm` is 2px for chips and ticks.
- Hairlines (`border-rail`) do exactly two jobs: they separate the rows of a
  register, and they mark where a part of a page begins. Everything else is
  separated by space. Never use one as decoration.
- Structure has two levels and no more. A section or page part is announced by
  `SectionHeader` (mono, 1.25rem, ink, optional count on the right); the rows
  under it are separated by hairlines. Do not invent a third level.
- Charts may keep a `border border-rail bg-surface` frame: that is one of the four
  sanctioned uses of `--surface`.

## Motion

- No entry animations. No `initial={{ y: ... }}`, no `delay: index * 0.1`, no
  staggering. Content is present on the first frame.
- `FadeIn` from `components/motion-wrapper.tsx` is opacity-only and takes no
  `delay` or `direction` props any more. `StaggerContainer` and `StaggerItem` no
  longer exist.
- Hover transitions are colour only, 120 to 150ms. No translate, no scale.
- `ThemeProvider` already wraps everything in `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`.

## Recharts

Recharts writes `fill` and `stroke` as SVG presentation attributes, where
`var()` is not reliable. Use the classes defined in `app/globals.css` instead:

`chart-ink`, `chart-ref`, `chart-limit` (fills) and `chart-ink-stroke`,
`chart-ref-stroke`, `chart-limit-stroke`, `chart-rail-stroke` (strokes).

Apply them via the `className` prop on `Bar`, `Cell`, `ReferenceLine`, and via
`tick={{ className: "chart-ref" }}` on axes. For `Tooltip`, `contentStyle` is an
inline style object and does support `hsl(var(--surface))` directly.

Chart semantics follow the page: bars in `--ink`, axes and ticks in `--ref`,
reference lines for limits in `--limit` dashed, grid lines in `--rail`.
Do not colour bars by index with a rainbow array.

## Accessibility floor

- Nothing below 11px (`text-micro` is 0.6875rem = 11px).
- Interactive targets are at least 44px in both dimensions, padding counts.
- Keyboard focus is handled globally by `*:focus-visible`; do not override it.
- Anything purely decorative gets `aria-hidden="true"`.

## Writing

No em dashes, no emoji, no glyphs outside Latin-1. Units are spelled as they read
("GB/s", not "GB/S"). Sentence case for prose, lowercase for nav and controls.
